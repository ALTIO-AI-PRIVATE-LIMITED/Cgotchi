How it works

  1. Flash writes the touch test firmware. Pick the usbmodem port.
  2. Track starts recording the log. The screen shows TOUCH DIAG. If Track finds nothing, unplug and replug once, then press Track again.
  3. Touch and swipe all over the screen for about 15 seconds. Tap the result buttons.
  4. Unplug. The log is saved with its diagnosis and the page resets for the next device.

Flashing replaces the firmware on the device. Reflash the normal firmware before shipping.
